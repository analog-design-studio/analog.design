roam for iPhone · iOS 26 and later

roam is the simplest alarm clock ever, and it doesn't jolt you awake. Alarms belong to places: set one in a time zone and it rings only while you're there.

Spin the globe to a zone, tap a time, and that's your alarm. Land in Tokyo and your Tokyo alarms wake up; your Los Angeles ones go quiet until you're home. No account, no server, no analytics — everything stays on your phone.

Features

  • Pick a zone by spinning a globe or typing a city name.
  • Tap a time for a one-off alarm today or tomorrow, or pick a specific date.
  • Press and hold a time for two seconds to make it repeat. If no days are chosen yet, it starts on Monday to Friday.
  • Repeats can run weekly, every other week, every third week, or monthly (for example, the second Tuesday of each month).
  • One-offs are white. Repeats come in three colors (orange, cyan and purple), and you can name what each color is for in settings.
  • Name any alarm. The name appears when it rings.
  • Swipe left to see every alarm across all zones, grouped by color.
  • Snooze for 7.5 minutes, as many times as you like. The countdown shows on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island.
  • Choose from eight alarm tones. The sound starts quiet and builds over about sixteen seconds to half volume. Turn on BOOST in settings to let it climb to full volume.
  • Switch to a 24-hour clock in settings.
  • Alarms are matched by clock time, so a Los Angeles alarm can ring in Phoenix when both share the same hour.

Support

  • Don't rely on roam as your only alarm for anything important. Set a backup.
  • Delete an alarm by tapping its pill on the grid or swiping its row in the tray. One-off alarms clear themselves after ringing.
  • To clear every alarm in a zone, pull the time grid down from the top and let go.
  • Alarms in zones you haven't been in for three months expire automatically.
  • If an alarm doesn't ring, check that your phone is in its zone and that roam has permission to schedule alarms.
  • After changing time zones, open roam so it can re-check your zone.
  • Bug reports and zone issues go to helloanalogdesign@gmail.com.
