LODGE

LODGE for iPhone · iOS 17 and later

LODGE is a private logbook for fishing, hunting, foraging and trapping. Log each session: where you went, what you took, the gear you used and the story. Keep your spots on a private map, plan trips with your crew, track what rides in the truck, and ask your own logbook questions in plain words. No account. Your logbook is stored only on your iPhone.

LODGE is a logbook, not a rulebook. It doesn't know the law, the seasons, the limits, the weather or the water where you are, and it can't tell you what is safe to eat. Those are always yours to check.

Requirements: iPhone with iOS 17 or later. Written answers in Find need iOS 26 or later and an iPhone with Apple Intelligence turned on. Without it, Find shows the matching records instead. The price is shown on LODGE's App Store page.

Support

Read this first

  • LODGE records what you tell it. It doesn't know or check any hunting, fishing, foraging or trapping rule: seasons, limits, sizes, licenses, tags, legal methods, closed areas or protected species. Check the current regulations for where you are, every time.
  • The limit on a harvest is a number you type. "Limit reached" only means you reached your own number.
  • The species list is a list of names. A species being listed doesn't mean it's legal to take, in season, found near you or safe to eat. The silhouettes are decoration and can't be used to identify anything.
  • Never eat a wild mushroom, plant, fish, shellfish or animal because of anything in LODGE.
  • The spot scores are a rough guess about when a spot might be good. They aren't a weather forecast, a tide table or a safety warning, and "Peak Window" never means a legal open season.
  • The maps are for marking your own spots. They aren't for navigation and don't show property lines, land ownership or closures.
  • LODGE isn't a safety device. It can't call for help and it doesn't share your location with anyone.

Getting around

  • Five tabs: Field, Spots, Logbook, Truck and Find. Tapping a tab always returns to that tab's main page.
  • Your profile is at the top left. The plus at the top right adds a Session, Highlight, Spot, Trip or Gear.
  • A new install starts empty. On first launch, LODGE shows its warnings and asks you to accept the privacy policy and terms. The date you accepted is shown at the bottom of Profile. If the terms change, LODGE asks you to accept again.

Logging a session

  • There are four steps: where, the take, the gear and the story.
  • Where. Choose fishing, hunting, foraging or trapping, the date (today or earlier), and one of your saved spots. Or choose "Somewhere new" and type a region.
  • The take. Open Harvest, search, and pick what you took. Fish, birds and animals are counted. Anything foraged is weighed in pounds. Type what you took and, if you want, the day's limit. You need at least one harvest with a take above zero to go on.
  • The harvest list shows what the catalog holds for the part of the Americas your spot is in. A species that isn't in the catalog can't be logged in this version.
  • The gear. Pick what you used from the truck, or add new gear on the spot. This step can be skipped.
  • The story. A caption and a rating from 1 to 10 are needed to save. Notes and up to 8 photos are optional. The first photo is the cover.
  • A Highlight is a session with a badge, such as Personal best or First of the season.

Spots

  • To add a spot, give it a name, pick what it's for, and drag the map until the crosshair sits on the place. LODGE never uses your iPhone's location. A spot is wherever you put the crosshair.
  • Notes, Best when and Access are your own words.
  • A spot's rating is the average of the ratings on the sessions you logged there.
  • Globe view: drag to turn, pinch to zoom, tap a pin. Slide left for where you've been in the past year. Slide right for the next 10 days.

Spot scores and Peak Window

  • Each spot gets a rough score from four things: time of year, temperature, rain and the moon's phase.
  • For the next 10 days LODGE uses Apple Weather. On a day with no Apple data, it shows long-term monthly averages for a broad climate zone, labeled as averages.
  • "Peak Window" is LODGE's own starting guess for when a kind of spot is usually good. It is the same for every new spot of that kind and has nothing to do with legal seasons.
  • LODGE shows the moon's phase. It gives no tide information, so use a real tide table.

Trips

  • Make a trip with a name, a region and your crew's names. Then tap each day on the calendar, plan each day, connect your spots, list the gear to bring, and log sessions from the trip.
  • Crew are only names. Nothing is sent to them.
  • Share builds a one-page trip sheet and saves it to Photos, in a phone shape or a letter page. "Show spot names" starts on. Turn it off and spots appear as their region only. Pins and coordinates are never on the sheet.

Truck

  • Gear rides under six headings: Fishing, Hunting, Foraging, Trapping, Camping and 4Wheeling.
  • Open a piece of gear to add photos, change its note or heading, and see every session it was used on.
  • Tap the truck under a post when an outing shows you need something. It then appears under Need New Gear.

Field and Logbook

  • The Field shows your sessions, highlights and trips, newest first. The star, heart and truck under a post file it under Priority, Favorites or Gear.
  • The Logbook shows this year's sessions by activity, your totals by species, and Dream Harvest.
  • Dream Harvest is a personal checklist of the 861 species in the catalog. A species is ticked the first time you log it. Tap a row to tick or untick it by hand. The list includes species that are protected or closed in many places, so a row on the list is never a suggestion to take one.

Find

  • Ask a question in plain words, such as "What gear did I use at" a spot. LODGE searches only your own sessions, spots, trips and gear.
  • On an iPhone with Apple Intelligence turned on (iOS 26 or later), Apple's on-device model writes a short answer from the matching records. On other iPhones, LODGE shows the closest matches. The line under the question box says which you're getting.
  • Written answers can be wrong. Every answer lists the records it came from, so tap them to check.
  • Find understands time phrases such as "last year", "2 years ago", "in October" and "last fall".
  • The share icon sends the question and answer as plain text. That text can include spot names, so read it before you send it.

Photos

  • Photos come from Apple's photo picker, so LODGE only gets the photos you pick. It keeps a smaller copy and leaves the original alone.
  • Tap a photo to view it, make it the cover or delete it. Tap your profile picture to change it.

Sharing a session

  • Tap Share, pick the platform and the post shape, and LODGE builds an image at that size and saves it to Photos. You post it yourself. LODGE never posts anything.
  • Spot names and pins are never on a session image. "Show region" controls whether the region line appears.
  • The first time, your iPhone asks whether LODGE may add to Photos. If you see "Couldn't save", open Settings, then Privacy & Security, then Photos, and let LODGE add photos.

Editing and deleting

  • On a saved session you can change the gear used, the photos, the cover and its lists. The caption, notes, date, take, rating and place can't be edited in this version. To fix one, delete the session and log it again.
  • On a saved spot you can change photos only. On a trip you can change days, plans, connected spots, crew and gear. On gear you can change the note and heading.
  • Delete a session, spot or trip from the menu at the top right of its page. Deleting can't be undone.
  • Deleting a spot or a trip keeps the sessions logged there. Deleting a session removes its take and photos. Dream Harvest ticks stay until you untick them.

Units and coverage

  • Weights are in pounds and temperatures in Fahrenheit. There is no metric option in this version.
  • The species catalog covers North, Central and South America.

Your data

  • Everything is stored only on your iPhone and is included in its iCloud or computer backup. There is no account, no sync between devices and no export in this version.
  • If you delete LODGE, everything in it is deleted too. We have no copy and can't recover it. Keep your iPhone backed up.
  • If LODGE can't open your saved logbook, it tells you at launch. Don't delete the app. Email us first.

Troubleshooting

  • "Lodge couldn't open your saved logbook." LODGE has started a new, empty logbook. The old one is still on your iPhone, and deleting the app would remove it. Don't delete LODGE. Email us with your iPhone model and iOS version. Restoring your iPhone from a backup made before the message first appeared is the other way back.
  • "Lodge can't save to this phone right now." Nothing you add will be kept while this message keeps appearing. Close LODGE fully and open it again. If it comes back, restart your iPhone, make sure it has free storage, and email us.
  • The map is blank. Maps need an internet connection. So does Apple Weather. Without it, spots show monthly averages. Everything else works offline.
  • Find only shows matches. Written answers need iOS 26 or later and Apple Intelligence turned on in Settings. Apple's model can take a while to download after you turn it on.
  • I can't get past a step. Step 1 needs a spot or a region. Step 2 needs a take above zero. Step 4 needs a caption and a rating.

What we collect

  • Nothing. LODGE has no accounts, no servers of ours, no analytics, no ads and no third-party code. We can't see anything in your logbook.

Location

  • LODGE never asks for Location Services and never reads your iPhone's GPS position. A spot's location is the point you drag the map to.

Maps

  • The map on a spot's page and in the new spot form is Apple Maps. To draw it, your iPhone asks Apple for the map of the area on screen. That tells Apple which area is being viewed, along with ordinary network details such as your IP address. Unlike the weather request, this is the real area around the spot, not a rounded one. Apple handles this under its own privacy policy.

Weather

  • For the 10-day outlook, LODGE asks Apple's weather service for the weather at each saved spot. Before it asks, LODGE rounds the spot's position to about a kilometre. The request contains that rounded position and nothing else from your logbook. Apple handles it under its own privacy policy.

Photos

  • LODGE uses Apple's photo picker, so it only receives the photos you choose. It can't browse your library.

Find and Apple Intelligence

  • Find searches your logbook on your iPhone.
  • When Apple Intelligence is on, your question and a few matching records are given to Apple's on-device language model to write the answer. This happens on your iPhone. We never receive your questions or answers.

Sharing
