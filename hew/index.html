Hew for iPhone · iOS 17 and later

Hew puts goals, habits and tasks in one list. Set a goal, attach the habits and tasks that get you there, and check them off each day. Hew shows your streaks, a calendar of what you did, and insights over any time frame. No account. Nothing leaves your phone.

Requirements: iPhone with iOS 17 or later. Reordering groups needs iOS 18 or later. The price is shown on Hew's App Store page.

Support

Getting started

  • A new install starts empty. Pull down on the list to add your first goal, habit or task.
  • A habit repeats daily, on weekdays, on weekends, or a set number of times a week. A task is done once.
  • Goals always have a color family, such as Health, Work, Family or Travel. A habit or task can have one too, or stay white in the Unassigned group. Attach a habit or task to a goal and it takes that goal's color.

Every day

  • Slide a habit or task right to complete it.
  • Slide left to Edit, Pause or Delete. Tap the row to close the buttons.
  • After you delete something, tap Undo in the message that appears. Only the most recent delete can be undone.
  • A paused habit doesn't count against your streak until you resume it.

Goals

  • Open the Goals tab to see your goals and what's attached to each one. Mark a goal achieved and it moves to the ACHIEVED fold.

Order

  • Press and hold any item to lift its whole color group, then drag it up or down. Completed items can't be lifted. This needs iOS 18 or later.

Calendar and Insights

  • Flip to the calendar to see past days. You can't mark a day that hasn't happened yet.
  • Insights shows goals reached, tasks done, longest streaks and your mix by color family. Pick a time frame, from this week to all time.

Your data

  • Everything is saved only on your iPhone and included in its iCloud or computer backup. Hew doesn't sync between devices yet.
  • If you delete Hew, everything in it is deleted too, unless you restore from a backup. Hew can't recover lost data for you.
  • Reminders, notifications, widgets, iPad and iCloud sync aren't available yet.
