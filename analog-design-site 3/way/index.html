Way for iPhone · iOS 17 and later

Color your way.

Way is an iPhone color tool. Point the camera at a color, or upload a photo. Way reads the color, names it, and shows ten harmonies with HEX and RGB codes. Save colors and palettes into Projects. No account. Nothing leaves your phone.

Requirements: iPhone with iOS 17 or later. Way is a one-time purchase on the App Store, not a subscription. For refunds, go to Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Support

Getting an accurate read

  • Shoot in even light without glare or deep shadow.
  • For the most accurate color, use Set White on a white or gray area in the photo.
  • The square in the viewfinder covers exactly the area Way reads, and a captured photo opens on that color.
  • If Way picked the wrong color, view the other candidates or touch a different spot. Pinch with two fingers to zoom in (up to 8x) and move the photo. When you're zoomed in, a touch reads a smaller area. Tap 1X to reset.

Camera

  • If the camera doesn't open, turn on camera access for Way in Settings. Upload works without it.
  • Camera zoom, flash and switching cameras aren't available yet.

The wheel

  • The Light (RGB) wheel suits screens. The Paint (RYB) wheel suits pigment. In Paint mode the codes stay HEX and RGB.
  • Undertone mixes your color with white, gray or black. Opacity sets how much, and stays off until you pick an undertone.
  • Search the wheel by HEX or RGB code.

Library

  • Save a single color from the results page, or a palette from the wheel. Everything you save needs a name.
  • The switch at the top shows Colors, Palettes or Projects. With none chosen, you see everything. Tap the chosen one again to go back to everything.
  • Tap the plus in the header to make a Project. A Project holds any number of colors and palettes. Each color or palette is in one Project or in none.
  • Swipe a row left to Copy it under a new name, Move it into a Project, or Delete it. When the Library is showing everything, you can also hold and drag a color or palette onto a Project.
  • A color saved from a photo keeps that photo, with a marker where you picked it. A palette made from that color gets its own copy. You can remove the photo.
  • Your Library is included in your iPhone backups. It doesn't sync between devices.

Sharing

  • Tap a code to copy it. Share sends a palette as text.

Contact

  • Email helloanalogdesign@gmail.com with "Way" in the subject. Include your iPhone model and iOS version. If a color read wrong, attach the photo.

Privacy policy

Effective 19 November 2026. Replaces the version of 11 November 2026.

  • Way has no accounts, no servers, no analytics and no ads. The app makes no network connections.
  • Photos are read on your iPhone and are never sent anywhere.
  • Way only gets the photos you choose. It doesn't get the rest of your photo library. Using the camera is optional.
  • Your colors, palettes, Projects and any source photos you keep are stored only in Way on your iPhone. You can remove a source photo, or delete any item, at any time.
  • That data is included in your iPhone's iCloud or computer backup, under your Apple Account. Way doesn't sync it between devices.
  • Way doesn't collect location, tracking data or personal information from anyone, including children.
  • If you've chosen to share data with developers, Apple may give us anonymous totals of downloads and crashes.
  • If you email us, we use your email only to reply.
  • If a change affects your privacy, we'll post it here with a new effective date before it ships.
