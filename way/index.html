Reading a color

The color doesn't match what I see.

Light changes color. On the "Is this the color you meant?" screen, use Set White and touch something white or gray in the photo. Way uses it to take the tint of the light out of the reading. Even light, no glare and no shadow give the best result.

Way picked the wrong color.

Tap No to see the other colors it found, or touch the photo and drag the marker to the exact spot.

What do High, Medium and Low mean?

How sure Way is of the reading. If you get Low, move closer and try again in more even light.

Camera

Way says it doesn't have camera access.

Tap Open Settings and turn Camera on. Upload works without it.

Can I zoom, use the flash or switch cameras?

Not yet. Move the phone until the color fills the square.

Harmonies and the wheel

What is Light vs. Paint?

Two color wheels. Light (RGB) is the wheel screens use, where the opposite of red is cyan. Paint (RYB) is the painter's wheel, where the opposite of red is green. Use Light for screens and Paint for pigment.

What do undertone and opacity do?

They mix your color with white, gray or black. The slider sets how much of the color is in the mix, and the line under it states the mix, for example "70% COLOR OVER 30% WHITE".

Can I start from a color code?

Yes. Open Wheel, tap search and type a HEX or RGB code.

Palettes

How do I save a palette?

Open a harmony on the wheel and tap Save. From a photo result, tap a harmony first to open it on the wheel.

How do I delete one?

Swipe it in the Palettes list.

Do palettes move to a new iPhone?

They are part of your iPhone backup, so restoring from a backup brings them along. Way does not sync between devices.

How do I get the codes out?

Tap a code or a color to copy it. Share sends every color in the palette, with HEX and RGB, as text.

Privacy Policy

Effective November 11, 2026

Way is a color tool for iPhone made by analog design. The short version: Way has no accounts, no servers, no analytics and no ads. Everything it does happens on your iPhone, and we never receive any of it.

Your photos

When you capture or upload a photo, Way reads its colors on your iPhone. The photo is never sent anywhere. Way does not keep a copy once the color is read, with one exception: a saved palette can keep a smaller copy of the photo its color came from, so the color and its source stay together. That copy is stored inside Way on your iPhone, and you can delete it from the palette at any time. Way never adds anything to your photo library.

Camera

Way asks for camera access so you can point your iPhone at a color. The live view and the photos you capture are processed on your iPhone only. You can turn camera access off at any time in the Settings app. Upload still works without it.

Photo library

Upload opens Apple's photo picker. Way receives only the photo you choose and cannot see the rest of your library.

Palettes

Palettes you save, and any photos kept with them, are stored in files inside Way on your iPhone. Way does not upload or sync them. If you back up your iPhone with iCloud or a computer, your palettes and their photos are part of that backup. Apple handles the backup under your own Apple Account, and we have no access to it.

To delete a palette, swipe it in the Palettes list. Deleting a palette deletes its photo. Deleting Way removes everything.

Sharing and copying

When you tap Share, Way hands the color codes to the iPhone's share sheet as plain text. Where that text goes is up to you and the app you pick. Copying a code puts it on your clipboard.

Purchases

Way is bought on the App Store. Apple takes the payment, and we never see your card or billing details.

The purchase belongs to your Apple Account, not to an account with us. Apple gives us sales totals that do not identify you.

What we don't collect

  • No account, sign-in, name or email address
  • No location
  • No analytics, usage tracking or advertising
  • No tracking across other apps or websites
  • No outside code that sends data anywhere

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone settings, Apple may show us anonymous download and crash totals. Those come from Apple, not from Way.

Children

Way does not collect personal information from anyone, children included.

If you email us

If you write to us, we receive your email address and whatever you send. We use it only to reply, and we don't add it to any list.

Your rights

Because Way collects no personal data, we hold nothing about you to access, correct, export or delete. Your palettes and photos stay on your iPhone, under your control.

Changes

If Way changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this page and its date before the change ships.
