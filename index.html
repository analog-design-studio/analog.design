LODGE for iPhone · iOS 17 and later

LODGE is a private logbook for fishing, hunting, foraging and trapping.

Log each session: where you went, what you took, the gear you used and the story. Keep your spots on a private map, plan trips with your crew, track what rides in the truck, and ask your own logbook questions in plain words. No account. Your logbook is stored only on your iPhone.

LODGE is a logbook, not a rulebook.

It doesn't know the law, the seasons, the limits, the weather or the water where you are, and it can't tell you what is safe to eat. Those are always yours to check.
